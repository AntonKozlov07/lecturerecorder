Record from

For Zoom, Teams or a video. After you press Start, choose what to share:

  • Zoom or Teams app: pick Entire screen and turn on Share system audio.
  • A meeting or video in a browser tab: pick that tab and turn on Share tab audio.

Only the sound is recorded, not the screen. To stop, press Stop here or Stop sharing in the browser bar. Use headphones when recording both, so the microphone doesn't pick up the meeting a second time. On a Mac, sharing a browser tab is the most reliable way to capture audio.

Audio is transcribed on this computer as you record. Keep this window open while recording.
